Create a blog describing  My experience of shingles.

* My sister is an RN. My dad was a hospital administrator and I was pre-med for a bit. While I am no medical professional. I'm familiar with the healthcare system and have a general understanding of medical conditions. So when I started experiencing symptoms, I had a pretty good idea of what was going on.
My godmother had shingles and I remember her killing me how miserable it was. So when I was 49, I asked for the vaccine, but was told I could not get it without doctor's approval. So I decided to wait. I turned 50 in March and I got shingles in April and I spent all of May miserable from it.
* I noticed some blisters near my eyebrow while camping and Initially thought it was Poison Ivy. And I did a teledoc session. And they gave me some steroid cream.
* The next day I realized this was not poison ivy, So I scheduled a a appointment with an ophthalmologist and my PCP.
* My ophthalmologists took it very seriously and informed me that my vision was at risk.  And if I ever felt something on the tip of my nose, then to immediately call him at any hour. Because that meant that it had reached the end of my nerves and was likely already in my eye.
*  I a few days later the pain was intense at a seven to eight level. And went on for multiple days.
* My eye had gotten so swollen that it was completely shut. And It felt like I had lightning shooting through my head every few seconds. It was so much that I would even start to twitch at times.
* At this point I was miserable and desperate and willing to try anything. So I then did some research for an acupuncturist.
*  I was amazed at how quickly I felt better as my pain went from a seven to eight down to a four to five in that session
* He prescribed me some herbs and a few days later I was back for my next session, which took the pain that had crept up a bit. Back down to a three to four.
*  I was so grateful to to be feeling better within the next wave of problems hit
*  I felt better, but the lightning which had abated had then started to become a lot more consistent and wasn't just on my skull, but had hit my eyebrows and eyelids.
* I was unable to sleep and for about four days I went to bed at four AM and then was waking up again throughout the night.
* By the fifth day I was an emotional wreck and went back and told my doctor about this.
* They then upped the dosage of gabapentin and gave me something for sleep.
*  The first night I still only got six hours of sleep, but it was better than the four restless ones.
* The next night I slept for 10 hours and felt human for the first time in almost a month.
* I'm so grateful to be able to get better sleep and feel like I'm able to Do my normal activity levels.
* When I finally got over the hump, I realized how absolutely angry and grumpy I was from everything and how I was not exactly  Miserable to be around, but most definitely edgy.
* I also heard so many other people's stories that while my experience was a month of misery, I couldn't imagine hearing about some of the other experiencing some of what other people had.  For example, someone had it in their mouth, another in their Intestants and worst of all someone got it in their vagina. But was too embarrassed to say anything about it until days later. And they think that might have led to that person's dementia.
	In retrospect, I'm still frustrated that I wasn't able to get my vaccine in time. But I rate this journal in hopes that someone else might see it and help save themselves.
